The write statistics now expose the queueing delay a setpoint suffers before its send even starts. The round-trip time was measured once the writer already held the connection, so the wait between write() handing a buffer over and the writer picking it up, the part of delivery latency that actually reaches the PLC late, was invisible. Each pending buffer is stamped at handoff; ads_write_buffer_age_ms publishes its age when the send starts and ads_write_handoff_latency_ms the full handoff-to-send-complete latency.
