Optional `setpoint_sequence_plc_symbol` and `setpoint_time_plc_symbol` hardware parameters annotate every streamed write with a UDINT sequence number and an LREAL steady-clock timestamp in seconds. Both are packed into the same sum-write transaction as the setpoints they describe, so they change if and only if the accompanying targets are new. A PLC interpolating between streamed samples can then detect a genuinely new setpoint even when consecutive setpoints are identical, and place it on the time axis free of ADS transport jitter. The sequence counts controller write cycles, not transmitted buffers: a coalesced buffer shows up as a jump, telling the PLC how many updates it lost. Wrapping at the 32-bit boundary is expected. Absent parameters leave the behaviour unchanged. (setpoint-sequence-timestamp)
